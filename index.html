See your day as a timeline, not a list.

Most prayer apps dump five times on a screen and leave you to it. Mawaqif shows your whole day as a journey — with a live countdown, and a quiet invitation to stop when a prayer arrives.

1

Live prayer timeline

Fajr through Isha on a single horizontal line. See where you are in the day at a glance.

2

Prayer Focus Mode

When the adhan hits, Mawaqif blocks distracting apps so the moment gets your full attention.

3

Reflection & Qada

Privately log each prayer. Track make-up prayers without guilt or public streaks.

How it works

One quiet moment, five times a day.

01
🕐

Prayer time approaches

Mawaqif quietly tracks the next prayer using your location and calculation method.

02
🔔

You get a gentle nudge

Not a jarring alarm — a calm heads-up that the adhan is coming in a few minutes.

03
🎯

Focus Mode activates

Distracting apps are blocked for the prayer window. Your phone becomes a tool for salah.

04
🌅

You return to your day

Apps unlock automatically. Optional reflection prompt: did you pray? Private, no guilt.

What's inside

Built for the way you actually pray.

🌍

Accurate anywhere

Auto-detects your country and applies the correct calculation method — Umm al-Qura, ISNA, MWL, Karachi, and more. High-latitude rules included.

🎯

Prayer Focus Mode

The feature almost no other prayer app has. Blocks distracting apps around each prayer so you can actually be present.

📊

Reflection & Qada

Log each prayer privately. Track make-up prayers in a separate list. Supportive tone, no shame.

🌙

Ramadan mode

When Ramadan arrives, Mawaqif shifts — suhoor & iftar countdowns, a fasting tracker, and dua reminders at iftar.
