Your Daily Stopping Points.

Mawaqif is a calm, clutter-free prayer times app that helps you stay present, not just reminded. Currently in closed testing — join the waitlist to be among the first to try it.

Why Mawaqif?

Timeline UI

See your day as a journey, not a list. Five prayers as checkpoints, with a live countdown to the next one.

Prayer Focus Mode

When it's time to pray, Mawaqif gently blocks distracting apps so you can reconnect with what matters.

Accurate Anywhere

Auto-detects your location and applies the correct calculation method for your region.

Reflection & Qada

Privately log your prayers and track make-up prayers. No guilt, no public shaming.

Ramadan Mode

Special theme, suhoor/iftar countdown, and a fasting streak tracker during Ramadan.

Calm Design

A distraction-free interface with a soft green, gold, and cream palette.
