Your Daily Stopping Points.

Mawaqif is a calm, clutter-free prayer times app that helps you stay present, not just reminded. Currently in closed testing — join the waitlist to be among the first to try it.

Why Mawaqif?

Timeline UI

See your day as a journey, not a list. Mawaqif shows your five prayers as checkpoints on a timeline, with a live countdown to the next one.

Prayer Focus Mode

When it's time to pray, Mawaqif gently blocks distracting apps so you can disconnect from the noise and reconnect with what matters.

Accurate Anywhere

Auto-detects your location and applies the correct calculation method for your region. Supports high-latitude rules and manual city search.

Reflection & Qada

Privately log your prayers and track any make-up prayers. No guilt, no public shaming, just a supportive companion.

Ramadan Mode

During Ramadan, the app transforms with a special theme, a suhoor/iftar countdown, and a fasting streak tracker.

Calm Design

A beautiful, distraction-free interface with a soft green, gold, and cream palette. Supports both light and dark mode.
